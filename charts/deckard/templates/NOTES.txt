Deckard is running as deployment/{{ include "deckard.fullname" . }} in namespace {{ .Release.Namespace }}.

Watch its scans:

  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} logs -f deployment/{{ include "deckard.fullname" . }}

A config Deckard cannot use stops it at startup with a message naming the problem, so a
pod in CrashLoopBackOff is almost always the config: read its logs.

Credentials come from Secret deckard-credentials. Changing it does not restart Deckard:

  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} rollout restart deployment/{{ include "deckard.fullname" . }}
